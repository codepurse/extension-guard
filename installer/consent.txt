Ward - what this installs and what it does

Ward is a parental-control / accountability tool. By installing it you
acknowledge and agree to the following:

1. This software enforces the restrictions configured on this device, using
   Windows enterprise policy and system-level filtering. Depending on what is
   configured, that can include: keeping browser extensions force-installed so
   they cannot be removed from the browser's normal Remove/Disable buttons;
   blocking websites; blocking chosen applications from running; blocking
   browsers it is unable to filter; forcing browser settings such as disabling
   private windows and requiring SafeSearch; and holding any of these to a
   schedule or a daily time limit.

2. If DNS filtering is enabled, this software points your browsers' DNS at
   Cloudflare for Families. Your name lookups are then answered by Cloudflare
   instead of your internet provider, under Cloudflare's terms.

3. It keeps a local activity log of its own actions - what was blocked, what was
   changed, when protection was paused. It does not record which pages you
   visited or what was on them, and nothing leaves this device.

4. It installs a background Windows service and a watchdog that restart
   themselves if stopped or killed. This is intentional: it is what prevents the
   protection from being switched off in a moment of weakness.

5. Removing this software requires the uninstall password set during this setup.
   That password should be held by the parent or accountability partner, not by
   the person whose browsing is being filtered.

6. You should install this only on a device you own or administer, or with the
   clear consent of the device's owner/user. Installing software that resists
   removal on someone else's device without their knowledge may be unlawful.

If you do not agree, choose "I do not accept" and setup will close.
